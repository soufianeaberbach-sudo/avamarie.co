WHO WE ARE

OUR
STORY

From a dream in England, to a force in global fashion manufacturing.

Chapter 01

WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

It started with two people and one impossible dream. A mother whose hands could transform the humblest fabric into a work of art — her stitches were invisible, her instincts immaculate, her craftsmanship whispered about across every tailor's circle in the city. And a father — a quiet force of nature — a businessman who could see opportunity where others saw only obstacles, who understood that the world didn't need more clothing; it needed better clothing, made by people who cared. Together, in a workshop no larger than a living room, they began to build something extraordinary. The neighbors heard the sewing machines humming late into the night. The fabric rolls stacked higher each month. Orders grew from local shops to regional chains. What started as a family's livelihood was quietly transforming into the seed of an empire. Neither of them could have imagined how far those humble stitches would travel.

Chapter 02

THE NEXT GENERATION

The children grew up not in playgrounds, but between cutting tables and fabric warehouses. They learned to read a pattern before they could read a book. Every school holiday was spent in the workshop — threading bobbins, organizing buttons, watching in awe as their mother's hands turned flat pieces of cloth into three-dimensional garments. By the time they were teenagers, each had developed a unique specialization: one understood production engineering, another mastered commercial strategy, one became a design prodigy, and the last inherited their father's visionary instinct for scaling businesses. The workshop had become a school of excellence, and its graduates would go on to build an empire that spans three continents.

Chapter 03

THE FIRST CORPORATE LEAP

In 2005, the family's eldest took the workshop's legacy and transformed it into a formal corporate entity in England. It was a bold move — competing against established manufacturers with decades of market presence. But they had something no competitor could replicate: an obsessive, almost religious devotion to quality passed down through generations. Within five years, premium British fashion brands were lining up. The company became one of England's most respected manufacturing groups, proving that the family's standard of excellence could scale without sacrificing a single stitch of quality. The small workshop had officially become a serious player in global fashion manufacturing.

Chapter 04

EXPANDING ACROSS CONTINENTS

The family's ambition knew no borders. By 2010, a new high-volume production facility was established in South Asia, capable of handling 120,000+ garments per month. It was proof that the family's manufacturing philosophy could transcend geography. Another sibling launched a fast-turnaround specialist operation in England, targeting trend-driven collections. The network was growing — not just in size, but in capability. Each company served a different market need, yet all shared the same DNA: uncompromising quality, relentless innovation, and the stubborn belief that there is no shortcut to excellence.

Chapter 05

AVA MARIE — THE CROWN JEWEL

In 2021, the most ambitious chapter was written. Ava Marie was founded in Tangier, Morocco — strategically positioned at the crossroads of Africa and Europe. Two state-of-the-art production facilities. A monthly capacity of 250,000 garments. 16 production lines with room to expand to 20+. It was the culmination of over four decades of manufacturing wisdom, distilled into a single, world-class operation. Ava Marie isn't just a factory — it's a statement. A declaration that premium fashion manufacturing belongs in Morocco, that Moroccan craftsmanship can rival any in the world, and that a family's dream, born in a tiny workshop, can reshape an entire industry. This is the crown jewel — and the story is far from over.

Step by Step

MANUFACTURING
JOURNEY

From concept to delivery — 10 stages of precision, expertise, and excellence.

Our 10-Step Process

FROM DESIGN TO
DELIVERY

Every stage is executed with precision, expertise, and the Ava Marie standard of excellence.

Step 01

DESIGN

Every great garment starts with a powerful idea. Our creative team works alongside your designers to develop, refine and visualize fashion concepts that push creative boundaries. We translate inspiration boards, trend reports and brand guidelines into production-ready designs — maintaining your brand DNA while elevating it to its fullest potential.

01
02

Step 02

PATTERN DEVELOPMENT

Our master pattern makers use advanced CAD software to transform designs into precise, graded production templates. Every seam allowance, dart and construction detail is meticulously engineered for both aesthetic perfection and manufacturing efficiency — ensuring flawless consistency from first sample to bulk production at scale.

Step 03

SAMPLE CUTTING & TESTING

Before committing to bulk production, we create precise prototypes for quality assessment and design validation. Each sample is rigorously evaluated for fit, construction integrity, material behavior and visual appeal — ensuring that when production begins, the result exactly matches your vision.

03
04

Step 04

MODIFICATION & APPROVAL

Your feedback drives every refinement. Our technical team adjusts fit, construction details and finishing techniques based on your sample review — continuing this iterative process until the sample perfectly meets your specifications. We move to production only when you are completely satisfied.

Step 05

QUANTITY & SIZE PLANNING

Strategic production planning is fundamental to commercial success. We collaborate with your merchandising team to develop optimal size distribution strategies, production ratios and quantity plans aligned with your sales data and market forecasts — maximizing sell-through rates while minimizing overstock.

05
06

Step 06

RAW MATERIAL PROCUREMENT

We source exclusively from our curated network of premium, certified fabric mills and component suppliers across Europe and Asia. Every material undergoes rigorous physical and laboratory testing before entering our facilities — because exceptional garments begin with exceptional materials.

Step 07

PRECISION CUTTING

Our automated CAD-driven cutting systems deliver millimeter-perfect accuracy across every single cut. Minimizing material waste while maximizing consistency, we achieve both environmental responsibility and impeccable quality control — each cut piece is verified against pattern specifications before assembly begins.

07
08

Step 08

SEWING & ASSEMBLY

Our skilled artisans and specialized sewing operators transform cut panels into finished garments using expert construction techniques. Inline quality control checkpoints verify seam strength, stitch density and construction alignment at every assembly stage — ensuring zero defective garments progress forward.

Step 09

FINISHING & QUALITY CHECK

Final impression is everything. Our dedicated finishing team applies professional steam pressing, precision trimming and comprehensive quality inspection to every single garment. Thread cleanliness, label placement, button security, and overall presentation are verified item by item.

09
10

Step 10

PACKAGING & DELIVERY

Your garments are packaged to your exact brand specifications — from custom hangtags and polybags to branded boxes with tissue paper. Our global logistics network ensures on-time delivery to any destination worldwide, with real-time shipment tracking and dedicated account management.
