Our Story

Founded in England with a vision to revolutionize fashion manufacturing, Ava Marie began as a humble dream in a small workshop. Like the great fashion empires before us, we understood that true success comes from unwavering commitment to quality and innovation.

In 2021, we expanded our operations to Morocco, establishing two state-of-the-art production facilities in Tangier. Today, our combined monthly capacity exceeds 32,000 garments, serving some of the world's most prestigious fashion brands.

Our journey from a small English startup to a major manufacturing partner for global brands is a testament to our dedication, craftsmanship, and relentless pursuit of excellence. We don't just manufacture clothing – we bring visions to life.
